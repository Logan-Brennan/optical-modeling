# Optical Modeling
Python tools to modeling the optical properties of nanoporous and multilayer thin films.

## Goals
- Transfer matrix modeling of thin film stacks
- Reflectance, transmittance, and absorptance calculations
- Effective medium approximations for porous materials
- Modeling illumination geometry dependent optical properties
- Validation with experimental integrating sphere measurements
- Optimization of EMA volume fractions to reproduce experimental values
- Jmax calculations combining absorptance and solar spectrum
